# Health and Safety Compliance

**Track:** Compliance & Ethics

## Purpose

This bundle gives every employee who handles regulated data, customer information or third-party relationships a practical grounding in the rules that keep the business inside its regulatory obligations. It combines short readings with worked examples so learners can apply the material in their day-to-day work.

## Audience

Every employee who handles regulated data, customer information or third-party relationships.

## Learning objectives

- Explain the core principles behind the rules that keep the business inside its regulatory obligations.
- Recognise common scenarios where the guidance applies.
- Apply the included checklist to a realistic situation.
- Know when to pause and ask for expert advice.
