# First Day Orientation

**Track:** New Hire Onboarding

## Purpose

This bundle gives new hires and the teammates who support their first weeks a practical grounding in the routines and expectations that make a new joiner productive quickly. It combines short readings with worked examples so learners can apply the material in their day-to-day work.

## Audience

New hires and the teammates who support their first weeks.

## Learning objectives

- Explain the core principles behind the routines and expectations that make a new joiner productive quickly.
- Recognise common scenarios where the guidance applies.
- Apply the included checklist to a realistic situation.
- Know when to pause and ask for expert advice.
