# Giving Effective Feedback - Course Content

## Module 1 - Foundations

Start with the why: the habits that help managers support strong, resilient teams. This module walks through the vocabulary, the situations you are most likely to meet, and the boundaries of your own role. Take note of the three checkpoints at the end of the module; they reappear in the scenario exercises.

## Module 2 - Practical scenarios

- Scenario A: a routine request that looks simple but touches the habits that help managers support strong, resilient teams.
- Scenario B: a deadline forces a shortcut; decide whether to take it.
- Scenario C: a colleague asks you to make a judgement outside your area.

For each scenario, write down who is affected, what evidence you would collect, and who to involve before acting.

## Module 3 - Check your understanding

1. In one paragraph, explain to a new colleague how the habits that help managers support strong, resilient teams applies to people managers and emerging leaders.
2. List the two signals that mean you should escalate rather than proceed alone.
3. Choose one scenario and describe the decision you would make, with reasons.

## Module 4 - Takeaways

Keep the quick-reference card nearby, and revisit the scenarios whenever your responsibilities change. If in doubt, ask before acting.
