# Giving Effective Feedback

**Track:** Leadership Development

## Purpose

This bundle gives people managers and emerging leaders a practical grounding in the habits that help managers support strong, resilient teams. It combines short readings with worked examples so learners can apply the material in their day-to-day work.

## Audience

People managers and emerging leaders.

## Learning objectives

- Explain the core principles behind the habits that help managers support strong, resilient teams.
- Recognise common scenarios where the guidance applies.
- Apply the included checklist to a realistic situation.
- Know when to pause and ask for expert advice.
